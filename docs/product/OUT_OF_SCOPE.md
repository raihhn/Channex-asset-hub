# Out of Scope

Do not implement these unless a later approved requirement explicitly changes scope:
- Asset purchase price, depreciation, or cost accounting
- Event budget and expense tracking
- Procurement and purchase-order workflow
- Vendor quotation/commercial workflow
- Event project planning, run-of-show, staffing, or task management
- Campaign ROI/performance analytics
- General document-management system
- Complex warehouse management such as bin-level WMS optimization unless later required
- A separate event master module solely to make AssetHub behave like an event-management platform

Booking may capture event/purpose/destination as context.
